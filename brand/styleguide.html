About — the llama, with a reason

The llama is a sticker here, not the site logo. One sentence explains the color.

About

The colors come from my llama logo — Andean textiles meet Peruvian chicha posters. Tradition and rigor underneath, something people want to look at on top. [sample copy — rewrite in your voice]

Do

  • Keep ~90% of the page calm; let the art frame the work.
  • Neon on black. Chullo reds on ivory.
  • Black text on any neon fill.
  • Put multi-series charts in night cards.
  • Animate only the one-time hero spray; keep the rest crisp.

Don't

  • Neon text or thin neon lines on ivory.
  • More than one spray per page or one llama per screen.
  • Recolor, stretch, crop or add paint to the llama.
  • Fade-in-on-scroll on every section.
  • Animate charts for style, or use ease-in.
